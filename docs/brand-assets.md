# External resource logos

These assets identify the destinations of the links labelled “Externe Ressourcen”
on the four Unternehmensentwicklung service pages. They do not represent VEYTRA
clients, press coverage, awards or partnerships.

- `public/brands/forbes.svg`: original Forbes wordmark SVG from the header at
  https://www.forbes.com/, retrieved 2026-09-07. Artwork preserved unchanged.
  The link points to https://www.forbes.com/entrepreneurs/.
- `public/brands/chatgpt.svg`: original black Blossom logo with a transparent
  background from https://cdn.openai.com/brand/OpenAI-Logos-2025.zip,
  retrieved 2026-09-07. The file
  `OpenAI-logos(new)/SVGs/OpenAI-black-monoblossom.svg` is preserved unchanged.
  The link points to https://chatgpt.com/.

Forbes is a mark of Forbes; ChatGPT and its logo belong to OpenAI.
